01

Information We Collect

We collect only the information that is strictly necessary to provide our services, process your orders, and improve your experience on Voltica Store.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (securely hashed).
  • Order Information: When you make a purchase, we collect your shipping address, billing address, phone number, and payment details (processed securely via Stripe).
  • Customer Support: When you contact us, we collect your name, email, and the content of your messages to respond appropriately.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: If you opt in, we collect your email address to send you product updates and promotions.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, and referral sources (via Google Analytics 4).
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location derived from your IP address (country and city level only).
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: See Section 4 for complete details.
🔒 We never store your credit card details. All payment processing is handled by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded or failed.
02

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal information exclusively for the following purposes:

A. Essential Business Operations

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Shipping products to your address
  • Processing payments and preventing fraud
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries
  • Managing your account and order history

B. Communication & Marketing

  • Sending order confirmations, shipping updates, and receipts
  • Responding to your questions and support requests
  • Sending marketing emails (only with your explicit consent — you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • Notifying you about important policy or service changes

C. Improvement & Analytics

  • Analyzing website traffic and user behavior to improve our services
  • Identifying and fixing technical issues
  • Developing new products and features
  • Conducting anonymous statistical research

D. Legal Compliance

  • Complying with tax, accounting, and legal obligations
  • Enforcing our Terms of Service
  • Preventing fraud and illegal activities
  • Responding to lawful requests from authorities
03

Data Sharing & Third Parties

✅ Voltica Store never sells your personal information. We do not rent, trade, or sell your data to third parties for their marketing purposes.

We share your information only with carefully selected service providers who help us operate our business, under strict contractual obligations to protect your data:

Service Provider Purpose Data Shared
Stripe Payment processing Payment details, billing address
Shipping Carriers Order delivery Name, shipping address, phone
Google Analytics 4 Website analytics Anonymized usage data, IP (anonymized)
Cloud Hosting Website infrastructure Technical logs (IP, user agent)
Email Service Transactional emails Email address, order details

All third-party providers are contractually bound to:

  • Use your data only for the specified purposes
  • Implement appropriate security measures
  • Comply with applicable data protection laws
  • Delete your data when no longer needed

Legal Disclosures

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with legal processes
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of Voltica Store, our users, or the public
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
04

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us provide a better experience. We use cookies for the following purposes:

A. Essential Cookies (Required)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They cannot be disabled.

  • Session cookies: Maintain your session while browsing
  • Cart cookies: Remember items in your shopping cart
  • Security cookies: Protect against CSRF attacks

B. Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These help us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it.

  • Google Analytics 4: Collects anonymized usage statistics
  • Performance metrics: Monitor site speed and reliability

C. Marketing Cookies (Optional)

These help us show you relevant content and measure campaign effectiveness.

  • Conversion tracking: Measure the success of our marketing
  • Remarketing: Show relevant ads on other platforms

Your Cookie Choices

You can control cookies through:

  • Our cookie consent banner (when available)
  • Your browser settings (block or delete cookies)
  • Industry opt-out tools (Digital Advertising Alliance, NAI)
  • Do Not Track browser signals (we honor DNT:1)
ℹ️ Disabling cookies may affect certain website features, such as keeping items in your cart or remembering your preferences.
05

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

Technical Safeguards

  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3
  • Data Encryption: Sensitive data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption
  • Secure Payment Processing: PCI DSS Level 1 compliant (Stripe)
  • Access Controls: Strict role-based access to personal data
  • Regular Security Audits: Ongoing vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
  • Two-Factor Authentication: Required for administrative access

Organizational Safeguards

  • Employee training on data protection
  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Confidentiality agreements with all staff and contractors
  • Incident response plan for potential data breaches
⚠️ While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but commit to promptly notify you of any breach affecting your data as required by law.
06

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Data Type Retention Period Reason
Account information Duration of account + 30 days after deletion request Service provision
Order history 7 years Tax and accounting requirements
Customer support messages 3 years after last contact Service quality and legal protection
Marketing preferences Until you unsubscribe + 30 days Respecting your choices
Server logs 90 days Security and troubleshooting
Analytics data 14 months (anonymized after) Business analysis

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it so it can no longer be associated with you.

07

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights under applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, etc.):

Your Rights

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing for marketing or legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time (for consent-based processing)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights (CCPA)

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at:

Privacy Requests
contact@volticastore.com

We will respond to your request within:

  • 30 days for GDPR requests (EU/EEA residents)
  • 45 days for CCPA requests (California residents)
  • 30 days for all other requests

We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request to protect your privacy and security.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

In addition to the rights above, California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is being collected and sold (we do not sell data)
  • Opt out of the "sale" of personal information (not applicable — we don't sell data)
  • Request disclosure of data collection and sharing practices
  • Designate an authorized agent to make requests on your behalf

European Union Residents (GDPR)

EU/EEA residents also have the right to:

  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority
  • Request information about the legal basis for processing
  • Request information about international data transfers
08

Children's Privacy

Voltica Store is not directed to children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 16 without parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at contact@volticastore.com.

👶 Our compliance includes the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and similar provisions under GDPR Article 8 in the European Union.
09

International Data Transfers

Voltica Store operates globally. Your personal information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States.

When we transfer data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

Safeguards We Use

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): EU-approved contractual terms for transfers outside the EEA
  • Data Processing Agreements: Contractual obligations with all third-party processors
  • Adequacy Decisions: Reliance on EU Commission adequacy decisions where applicable
  • Binding Corporate Rules: For intra-group transfers (where applicable)
  • Certifications: EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework participation (where applicable)

By using our services, you consent to the transfer of your data to countries that may have different data protection standards than your country of residence. We remain responsible for your data regardless of where it is processed.

To request a copy of the safeguards in place for international transfers, please contact us at contact@volticastore.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, legal requirements, or other factors.

How We Notify You

  • Material changes: We will notify you via email (if you have an account) and/or a prominent notice on our website at least 30 days before the changes take effect
  • Minor changes: We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

What Constitutes a Material Change

Material changes include, but are not limited to:

  • New uses of your personal information
  • Sharing your data with new categories of third parties
  • Changes to your rights or how to exercise them
  • Changes to data retention periods
  • Changes to international transfer safeguards

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of our services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
