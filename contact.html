04 — Frequently Asked

Quick answers to common questions.

Most questions can be answered here. For anything else, use the contact form or email us directly.

How quickly does Voltica Store respond to inquiries?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on business days (Monday-Friday, 9AM-6PM EST). For urgent matters regarding active orders, use subject line "URGENT: [Order Number]" for priority handling within 2 hours.

What information should I include in my message?

For fastest service, include your order number (from your confirmation email), the email address used for your order, a clear description of your question, photos or videos for damaged items, and your preferred resolution (refund, replacement, etc.).

How do I report a defective or damaged item?

Take photos or videos of the issue, then email contact@volticastore.com within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and visual documentation. We'll respond within 24 hours with a prepaid return label and arrange replacement or refund.

Does Voltica Store offer partnership or wholesale opportunities?

Yes! We're open to partnerships with content creators, reviewers, and select wholesale partners. Email contact@volticastore.com with subject "PARTNERSHIP INQUIRY" and tell us about your audience, platform, and collaboration ideas.

Can I suggest a product for Voltica Store to carry?

Absolutely! We love product suggestions from our community. Email your recommendations to contact@volticastore.com with details about the product, why you think it fits our curation philosophy, and your personal experience with it.

Where can I find answers about shipping, returns, and policies?

Visit our dedicated pages: Shipping & Returns for delivery times and return policies, Privacy Policy for data practices, and Terms of Service for complete terms and conditions.
