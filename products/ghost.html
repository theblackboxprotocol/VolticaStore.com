PRODUCT STORY

THE EXPERIENCE

Forty-eight grams. That is all that stands between your hand and the screen. The ATK Ghost is an ultralight gaming mouse built for players who want their aim to land the instant they think it — PAW3950 Ultra sensor, 42,000 DPI, Dual 8K polling and 0.163 ms end-to-end latency, in a hollow composite carbon fiber shell that looks as fast as it feels.

A shell engineered to disappear. The Ghost is molded in one piece from composite carbon fiber, with a hollow structure and a central skeleton inside that strengthens the sides while balancing the weight. The result is a mouse that weighs just 48 g, with the weight evenly distributed — no heavy spot, no drag, no fatigue at the end of a long session.

A sensor that never misses. The PAW3950 Ultra tracks at up to 42,000 DPI, 750 IPS and 50G acceleration, driven by the Nordic 54H20 flagship MCU. From the first flick to the final headshot, the sensor follows your hand exactly — fast swipes, micro-adjustments and everything in between.

Speed you can measure. The Ghost polls at Dual 8K — 8000 Hz wired and wireless — so your clicks and movements reach the game with just 0.163 ms of latency. Dual-mode 2.4G wireless and wired USB-C give you the freedom to play untethered or plug in for the lowest latency possible.

Grip that stays dry. A new nano coating works with the delicate texture of the body for anti-slip performance MAX — always dry and in good condition, even when the match heats up. The forged carbon texture adds a premium feel under your fingers, with an excellent weight-to-strength ratio you can feel.

Built for every hand. The Ghost is ambidextrous, with six buttons, ATK Custom Optical Switches, an optical scroll wheel and a dedicated DPI button. No RGB, no distractions — just the controls you need, exactly where your fingers expect them.

Wireless when you want it, wired when it counts. Play on 2.4G wireless for a clean desk, or plug in the Type-C cable for wired play and recharging. The Ghost is made for esports: light, fast and ready for every match.

Voltica quality-inspected before delivery. Every Ghost is checked before it ships, and Elite Pass members get 15% off. This is the mouse for players who refuse to lose a fight to their hardware.
