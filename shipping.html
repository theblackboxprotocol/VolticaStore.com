Order Processing

All orders are processed quickly and efficiently at our US-based fulfillment center.

Processing Times

  • Standard Orders: Processed within 1 business day
  • Orders Placed Before 2PM EST: Often ship same day (Mon-Fri)
  • Weekend Orders: Processed next business day (Monday)
  • Holiday Orders: May take 1-2 additional business days
  • Pre-Order Items: Ship on or around the listed release date

Order Confirmation

Once your order is placed, you'll receive:

  1. Order Confirmation Email — Sent immediately with order details
  2. Shipping Confirmation Email — Sent when your order ships, with tracking number
  3. Delivery Updates — Carrier notifications as your package moves
⏰ Business Days: Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. Orders placed on weekends are processed the following Monday.

Express Shipping Options (US)

International Shipping

International shipping rates vary by:

Final shipping cost is calculated and displayed at checkout before payment. You'll see the exact cost before completing your purchase.

Free International Shipping Promotions

We occasionally offer free international shipping promotions for orders over certain amounts. These will be clearly advertised on our website and applied automatically at checkout when eligible.

Order Tracking

Every order ships with full tracking so you always know where your package is.

How Tracking Works

  1. You'll receive a tracking number via email as soon as your order ships
  2. Click the tracking link or copy the number to the carrier's website
  3. Track your package in real-time from our warehouse to your door
  4. Sign up for SMS/email notifications for delivery updates

Tracking Availability

  • US Orders: USPS, UPS, or FedEx tracking (updates every 2-4 hours)
  • International Orders: DHL, FedEx International, or national postal service
  • All Tracking: Available 24/7 via carrier websites

Account Tracking

If you have a Voltica Store account, you can view all your orders and their tracking status in your account dashboard — no need to search through emails.

ℹ️ Tracking Delays: Allow up to 24 hours after shipment for the first tracking update to appear. International shipments may take 48-72 hours to show initial movement.
🌍

International Shipping

We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International customers enjoy the same quality products and customer service, with some additional considerations.

Customs, Duties & Taxes

International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes, and customs fees imposed by the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our shipping fees.

  • Import Duties: Calculated by your country's customs authority
  • VAT/GST: May apply based on local tax laws
  • Customs Handling Fees: Some carriers charge processing fees
⚠️ Important: Voltica Store has no control over these charges and cannot predict their amount. Contact your local customs office for more information before ordering.

Restricted Countries

We currently cannot ship to countries subject to US trade sanctions or embargoes. If your country is restricted, you'll be notified at checkout.

International Returns

International returns are accepted within 30 days, but return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item is defective or we made an error. Refunds are issued in USD to your original payment method.

Language & Currency

Our website is currently in English with USD pricing. Your bank or payment processor will apply their current exchange rate at the time of purchase.

Delivery Issues

If something goes wrong with your delivery, we're here to help resolve it quickly.

Lost Packages

If your package shows as delivered but you haven't received it, or tracking hasn't updated in 7+ days:

  1. Check with neighbors and household members
  2. Check safe locations around your property (porch, mailbox, garage)
  3. Contact the carrier directly using your tracking number
  4. If still not found, contact us at contact@volticastore.com

We'll file a carrier claim and either resend your order or issue a full refund at your choice.

Damaged Shipments

If your package arrives visibly damaged:

  1. Take photos of the damaged package and contents
  2. Keep all packaging — carriers may require inspection
  3. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos
  4. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund

Wrong Address Delivered

If the carrier delivered to the wrong address despite the correct address on your order:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of expected delivery
  • We'll work with the carrier to recover your package
  • If unrecoverable, we'll reship or refund your order

Incorrect Address Provided

If you provided an incorrect shipping address:

  • Before shipment: Contact us immediately to update (no charge)
  • After shipment: Carrier may charge a $15-25 redirection fee
  • If returned to us: We'll reship for actual shipping cost
📞 Quick Action Required: Report delivery issues within 48 hours for fastest resolution. The longer you wait, the harder it becomes to file carrier claims.

Return Policy

We stand behind every product we sell. Our 30-day satisfaction guarantee lets you shop with confidence.

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

You may return most new, unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. If you're not completely satisfied, we'll make it right.

Return Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a return, items must be:

  • Returned within 30 days of the delivery date
  • In original, unused, resalable condition
  • In original packaging with all accessories, manuals, and documentation
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt)
  • Free of personal engravings, modifications, or damage

Non-Returnable Items

Certain items cannot be returned for health, safety, or digital content reasons:

  • Opened Software & Digital Content: Apps, digital downloads, software licenses
  • Personal Care Items: Earbuds with removed hygiene seals (health reasons)
  • Final Sale Items: Products marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance"
  • Custom Products: Personalized or engraved items
  • Gift Cards: Digital or physical gift cards
  • Perishable Items: Items with expiration dates (if applicable)
✓ Our Promise: If a product doesn't meet your expectations for any reason within 30 days, we'll work with you to find a solution — refund, replacement, or store credit.

Refund Timeline

We process refunds as quickly as possible once your return is received and inspected.

Refund Processing Timeline

Stage Duration What Happens
Return in Transit 3-7 business days Package travels back to our warehouse
Inspection 3-5 business days We verify condition and completeness
Refund Processing 1-2 business days Refund initiated to original payment method
Bank Processing 2-10 business days Your bank posts the refund to your account

Total Expected Timeline

  • Best Case: 7-10 business days from when you ship the return
  • Typical Case: 10-14 business days
  • Maximum: Up to 21 business days (international returns)

Refund Methods

Refunds are always issued to the original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Cards: 2-10 business days after processing (bank dependent)
  • PayPal: 1-3 business days after processing
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay: Same as underlying card
  • Gift Cards: Cannot be refunded; store credit issued instead

Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in cases of:

  • Items returned not in original condition
  • Missing accessories or packaging
  • Items returned after the 30-day window (at our discretion)
  • Visible signs of use beyond reasonable inspection
💡 Notification: You'll receive an email confirmation when your refund is processed. If you don't see the refund after 10 business days, contact your bank and then us at contact@volticastore.com.

Exchanges

How Exchanges Work

We do not process direct exchanges. Instead, we use a simple two-step process:

  1. Return the original item following our standard return process (Section 8)
  2. Place a new order for the item you want

Why This Approach?

  • Faster: You get your new item immediately, without waiting for the return to process
  • Flexible: You can choose any item, not just the same product in a different variant
  • Inventory Accurate: You see real-time stock levels when placing your new order

Exchange for Different Size/Color

If you need a different size, color, or variant of the same product:

  1. Place a new order for the variant you want
  2. Initiate a return for the original item
  3. We'll refund the original order once the return is processed

Out-of-Stock Items

If the item you want is out of stock, we can:

  • Notify you when it becomes available
  • Offer store credit for future use
  • Process a full refund to your original payment method
✓ Pro Tip: If you know you'll want a different variant, place the new order first while the item is in stock, then return the original. This ensures you don't miss out due to stock fluctuations.

Defective & Damaged Items

If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, we'll make it right — quickly and at no cost to you.

What Qualifies as Defective

  • Manufacturing Defects: Product doesn't work as described
  • Shipping Damage: Item arrives broken, cracked, or visibly damaged
  • Wrong Item: You received a different product than ordered
  • Missing Components: Essential parts are missing from the package
  • Dead on Arrival (DOA): Electronic items that don't power on

Resolution Options

For defective items, you can choose:

  1. Free Replacement: We ship a new unit at no cost (prepaid return for defective)
  2. Full Refund: 100% refund including original shipping charges
  3. Store Credit: 110% credit for future purchases (bonus for the inconvenience)

How to Report a Defective Item

  1. Document the issue: Take clear photos or videos showing the defect
  2. Contact us within 48 hours: Email contact@volticastore.com with order number, description, and photos
  3. Receive prepaid label: We'll email a free return shipping label within 24 hours
  4. Ship back the item: Use the prepaid label — no cost to you
  5. Get resolution: We'll ship your replacement or issue refund within 1-2 business days of receiving the defective item
⚠️ Report Quickly: Defective item claims must be filed within 48 hours of delivery. This helps us document issues with carriers and suppliers while evidence is fresh.

Manufacturer Warranty Claims

For issues that arise after the 30-day window but within the manufacturer's warranty period:

  • Contact us first — we'll help facilitate the warranty claim
  • Provide proof of purchase from Voltica Store
  • We'll coordinate with the manufacturer on your behalf
  • Most warranties cover 1-2 years from date of purchase
