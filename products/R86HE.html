THE EXPERIENCE

Every millisecond decides the round. The Attack Shark R86HE is a Hall Effect gaming keyboard built for players who want their input to land the instant they think it — 8000 Hz polling, 0.005 mm Rapid Trigger accuracy and 0.08 ms response latency, in a carbon fiber composite shell that looks as fast as it feels.

Magnetic switches change the game. Instead of a physical contact, the R86HE reads each keypress magnetically, so every press registers with the same precision, thousands of times over, with no debounce delay and no wear on the contact point. Rapid Trigger resets the key the moment you lift your finger — strafe, counter-strafe and repeat-peek with a speed a mechanical switch cannot match.

Speed you can measure. All keys poll at 8000 Hz, with a 256K scan sampling rate and 16K global sampling, so the keyboard tracks your hands at the pace of your game, not the other way around. From the first keypress to the on-screen action, the delay is just 0.08 ms.

Built like premium gear should be. The shell is a carbon fiber composite, the side flanks are CNC-machined aluminum with a geometric hollow-out design, and an aluminum wing reinforcement holds the whole board rigid. Inside, five layers of full filling absorb vibration and give every keystroke a deep, consistent feel.

Transparent carbon keycaps. The PBT keycaps are transparent with a carbon pattern, so the RGB light shines through every legend. A dedicated RGB side strip runs along the flank, turning the keyboard into a light source on your desk, not just a tool on it.

Your keyboard, your rules. The R86HE is fully programmable through the manufacturer's self-developed web driver — remap keys, tune the Rapid Trigger point, set macros and lighting, all from your browser. Anti-ghosting keeps every combination registered, media keys keep your sound one touch away, and the splash-resistant design shrugs off accidents.

Plug in and play. The R86HE connects by USB-C, wired for the lowest latency possible — no battery to charge, no wireless lag, no pairing. Adjustable tilt lets you set the angle that fits your hands, and the 86-key layout keeps the F-row and navigation cluster within reach without taking over your desk.

Voltica quality-inspected before delivery. Every R86HE is checked before it ships, and Elite Pass members get 15% off. This is the keyboard for players who refuse to lose a fight to their hardware.
